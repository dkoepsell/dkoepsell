## Core Research Trajectory

My work forms a unified research program, **The Architecture of Social Being**, developed across several major strands:

### 🧭 Relational Ethics
**[The Geometry of the Good](https://davidkoepsell.com/geometry-of-the-good.html)** (EthicsPress, 2025)  
A structural ontology of ethics that models obligation, trust, contradiction, and repair as *relational facts*, rather than norms, preferences, or commands. Ethical failure generates residue and repair obligations, which can be simulated and measured.

### ⚖️ Legal Ontology
**The Architecture of Justice** and **[A Structural Ontology of the Law](https://davidkoepsell.com/SOoL.html)** (Palgrave Macmillan, forthcoming)  
A realist, BFO-aligned account of legal systems that treats law as a structured network of roles, relations, contradictions, and institutional repair mechanisms. This work informs legal AI, IRAC-style reasoning tools, and formal legal modeling.

### 🤖 Ethics, AI, and Agency
A non-voluntarist account of agency grounded in **counterfactual accountability** and exposure to irreversible relations. This framework applies to humans, institutions, AIs, DAOs, and hybrid systems, and rejects intention or consciousness as necessary conditions for moral agency. The **Irreversible Accountability Test (IAT)** operationalizes the account.

### 🧬 Applied Formal Ontology
BFO-aligned ontology engineering applied beyond law: classification systems in medicine and psychiatry, historical philosophical systems, and the ontology of digital objects.

## Active & Representative Projects

### 🏛️ Legal Ontologies Foundry (LOF)
A new initiative, co-founded with William Mandrick and Barry Smith, to coordinate the development of interoperable, realist, BFO-aligned ontologies for the legal domain.

- Ontology Design Pattern specifications (LOF-ODP-001 through LOF-ODP-003)
- Validated OWL files accompanying each pattern
- A shared foundation for legal AI, compliance, and institutional modeling

> Legal ontologies that interoperate by design, not by accident.

---

### 🤖 BFO-Agent
A dialogue architecture that pairs language-model extraction with reasoner-validated commitments to build BFO-aligned OWL ontologies directly from source texts.

- Every ontology validated for logical consistency with the HermiT reasoner
- Per-class provenance linking concepts to the passages that produced them
- IAT computational study series testing accountability and reflexivity in language models

🔗 [Program overview](https://davidkoepsell.com/formal-ontology.html)

---

### 🔬 SimEthica
Agent-based simulation platform for testing ethical and ontological hypotheses.

- Models obligation issuance, fulfillment, denial, expiration, and repair
- Tracks contradiction debt, trust, moral energy, and epistemic agency
- Compares outcomes across nine normative regimes
- Adaptive mode adds heritable dispositions and emergent moral archetypes
- Rupture Predictor for identifying conditions that precede institutional collapse
- Exports CSV data for empirical analysis

> Ethical theory, but falsifiable.

🔗 [SimEthica Adaptive](https://davidkoepsell.com/SimEthica3d.html) · [Original](https://davidkoepsell.com/geometry-of-the-good-sim/) · [GoG + IAT Moral Reasoner](https://davidkoepsell.com/GoG-IAT-Reasoner.html)

## What Ties This Together

Across philosophy, law, ethics, and games, the core commitments are the same:

- Structure matters more than intention
- Obligation arises from relations, not rules
- Failure produces residue that must be repaired
- Agency is earned through accountability, not asserted
- Pluralism is not a problem to eliminate, but a condition to manage

If something cannot be modeled, traced, or tested, I treat that as a philosophical warning sign.

- ### 🏛️ Legal Ontologies Foundry (LOF)
A collaborative initiative, co-founded with William Mandrick and Barry Smith, to develop interoperable, logically well-formed ontologies for the legal domain. Modeled on the OBO Foundry: member ontologies share a common upper ontology (BFO, ISO/IEC 21838-2), a common format (OWL 2), persistent identifiers, and a common set of principles.

- **[LOF-ODP-001](https://legal-ontologies-foundry.github.io/)**: the base pattern the others import
- **[LOF-ODP-002: Legal Role Conferral](https://github.com/legal-ontologies-foundry/lof-odp-002)**: legal roles and the acts that confer and terminate them
- **[LOF-ODP-003: Correlative Deontic Positions](https://github.com/legal-ontologies-foundry/lof-odp-003)**: Hohfeld's eight fundamental legal positions and their correlative pairings
- **[LOF-ODP-004: Minimum Legal Chain](https://github.com/legal-ontologies-foundry/lof-odp-004)**: the chain through which a particular legal position comes to exist

> Legal ontologies that interoperate by design, not by accident.
